Welcome

The Clean Growth Tool helps users see the industrial structure of US regions, find feasible new industries, and identify clusters relevant to clean-energy growth. Use the tabs above to switch between views.

  • Geography Explorer — choose a region; see its complexity, top industries, and feasibility scatter.
  • Industry Explorer — choose a six-digit NAICS industry; see where it is strongest and where it is feasible.
  • Energy Technology — complexity-weighted feasibility for each technology subcategory; pulled from the illustrative crosswalk.
  • Industry Space — the network of co-located industries with a force-directed layout.
  • Peers & Heatmaps — pick an anchor region and compare it across its top peers.
  • About — methodology and the energy-technology crosswalk.
Note: The Energy Technology classification is illustrative and substantively under refinement. See the About tab for the full crosswalk.
